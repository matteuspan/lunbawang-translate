About this translator

Lun Bawang is spoken by roughly 50,000–80,000 people across the highlands of Borneo — Sarawak, Kalimantan and Brunei. This translator is free, and runs on a model fine-tuned on the first parallel corpus ever assembled for the language.

Translate in either direction, look up single words, and send a correction when the model gets something wrong. Corrections go back into the corpus.

Also known as Lun Dayeh or Lundayeh — this works equally as a Lundayeh translator, a Lundayeh dictionary, and for Lundayeh to English translation.
